How the price is worked out

  • Hours = area ÷ your clearing rate × the depth multiplier, plus walks, setup and travel
  • Cost = equipment + fuel + de-icer + labor + overhead
  • Price floor = (cost + card fixed fee) ÷ (1 − card fee % − target margin)
  • Suggested price = the floor rounded up to your step, never below your minimum charge

Questions

Does it work in Google Sheets?

Yes. It's a regular .xlsx file with standard formulas and no macros. Open it in Excel 2016 or newer, or in Google Sheets use File → Import → Upload.

Can I change the snow depth tiers?

Yes. The tiers and their time multipliers are an editable table on Settings.

What if my contract covers only some events?

Type how many events it covers on Season Compare. Extra events are billed at your per-push price.

Are card fees counted on seasonal contracts?

Yes. On Settings, choose whether contracts are paid by card or by cash or check. With card, the Season P&L charges the card fee on every contract payment.

Does it predict how much snow we'll get?

No. The low, normal and high event counts are numbers you type, for example from your own past seasons. The file uses no weather or snowfall data.
