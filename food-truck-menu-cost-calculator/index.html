How the numbers are worked out

  • Cost per usable unit = pack price ÷ (pack size × usable yield %), so trim and cooking loss are counted
  • Units convert with exact US definitions (1 lb = 453.59237 g, 1 cup = 8 fl oz), and grams per cup handles buy-by-weight, measure-by-volume
  • Food cost % = plate food cost ÷ menu price (packaging, labor and card fees are left out, and the file says so)
  • Suggested price = plate food cost ÷ target food cost %, rounded up to your step

Questions

Does it work in Google Sheets?

Yes. It's a regular .xlsx file with standard formulas and no macros. Open it in Excel 2016 or newer, or in Google Sheets use File → Import → Upload.

I buy by the pound but measure by the cup. Does that work?

Yes. Type grams per cup (or grams per each) on the ingredient's row and the recipe line converts.

What does food cost % include?

Only the ingredients and batch recipes in one serving, after yield. Packaging, labor, card fees and sales tax are left out of food cost % and counted separately in the Event Planner, because definitions differ and the file states which one it uses.

Can a batch recipe go inside another batch recipe?

No, one level only: batch recipes are built from ingredients, and menu items from ingredients and batch recipes. The row check tells you if you try.

Does it know what my ingredients cost?

No. Prices differ by supplier and week, so you type your own. Every number in the example file is made up.
