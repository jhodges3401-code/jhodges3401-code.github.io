Is it really pay once?

Yes. Every price on this page is a one-time payment. There is no subscription, no renewal and no hidden upgrade to keep using what you bought.

What if it isn't right for me?

Ask for a refund within 14 days of purchase by replying to your Gumroad receipt. No form, no interrogation.

Can I try before I buy?

Every paid product has a free live demo, and Desk, PeelPixel, the Quote Checker and the Rate Calculator have free versions you can keep using.

Where is my data stored?

On your device or in your own GitHub account. Desk, Proposeroo and PeelPixel run in your browser. Okayline and Kudoshelf are static files you host. We don't run a server that holds your data.

How do I receive the product?

Checkout is handled by Gumroad. You get an email receipt right away with your download, license key or setup steps. For Okayline and Kudoshelf you reply with your GitHub username and get invited to the private repo.

Do you offer team or business licenses?

Yes: Proposeroo Desk Team covers 5 people for $79 and Proposeroo Team covers 5 people for $49. Gumroad can generate an invoice from your receipt for expense reports.
