How the price is worked out

  • Load weight = cubic yards × your pounds per cubic yard for the material, plus any known extra weight
  • Cost = dump + special items + fuel + truck wear + labor + overhead
  • Price floor = (cost + card fixed fee) ÷ (1 − card fee % − target margin)
  • Suggested price = the floor rounded up to your step, never below your minimum charge

Questions

Does it work in Google Sheets?

Yes. It's a regular .xlsx file with standard formulas and no macros. Open it in Excel 2016 or newer, or in Google Sheets use File → Import → Upload.

Can I quote by cubic yards instead of truck fractions?

Yes. Pick a load size, or type exact cubic yards to replace it. Loads bigger than your truck count as extra dump trips.

My dump charges per load, not per ton. Does that work?

Yes. Choose per ton (with a minimum per visit) or a flat fee per load on Settings.

Can I rename the load sizes and materials?

Yes. Both are editable tables on Settings, including the pounds per cubic yard for each material.

Does it know what my local dump charges?

No. Dump fees and weights differ by place, so you type your own rates. Every number in the example file is made up.
