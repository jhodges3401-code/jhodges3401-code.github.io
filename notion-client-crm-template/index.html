When a client moves stage

From → toReady when…
Lead → DiscoveryThey replied and a call/async scope is booked or started
Discovery → ActiveScope + price agreed; kickoff scheduled; deposit received if you require one
Active → InvoicingMilestone accepted or period ended; invoice drafted
Invoicing → DonePaid in full (or write-off logged) and engagement closed
Any → PausedMutual hold; next check-in date set

FAQ

Questions, answered straight

Is there a free Notion client CRM template?

Yes, several, and some do more than ours. Templitize's Client CRM (Lite), Noel's Freelancer Client & Project OS and Recordwell's Simple Client CRM are all free in Notion's template gallery and work out follow-ups for you. If a CRM is all you want, start with one of those. This one is for people who also want the 15 client emails, the onboarding checklist and the written rules for when a client moves stage.

Does it work on Notion's free plan?

Yes. The Start Here guide says a free Notion account works: open the template link, click Duplicate and pick your workspace.

Is it a Notion link or files?

Both. The Start Here PDF has the one-click Duplicate link, and a zip holds the same system as six Markdown pages and two CSV databases, so you can import it yourself or keep it outside Notion.

Does it calculate anything for me?

No. The two databases are plain tables with no formulas and no links between them; you update Status and Next action date yourself. Notion can still filter, sort and sum a column. The worked example on this page shows the five quick filters the CRM guide suggests.

Does it come with a board view?

Not built in. The guide's setup step is to add a Board view grouped by Status in your copy, which takes a few clicks in Notion. The board in the listing image is a sample of that view.

Can I use it without Notion?

Yes. The Markdown files open in Google Docs, Obsidian or any notes app, and the CSV files open in Excel or Google Sheets.

Is it legal or accounting software?

No. It's an ops template for running clients, not a legal CRM, accounting software or tax advice. Use your own contracts.
