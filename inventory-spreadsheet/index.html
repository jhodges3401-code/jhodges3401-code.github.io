QR labels

How the QR codes work, and where they don't

Google Sheets and Excel 365

  • Each QR is drawn by the built-in IMAGE() function. No add-ons, scripts or macros.
  • In Google Sheets, click Allow access on the yellow banner once; until then the QR cells show a short note.

Older Excel (2016–2021)

  • The QR cells show a note instead. Click the QR image link on each row to open the QR as a picture, or print the Labels tab from Google Sheets.
  • The code text always prints, so every label stays readable.

The QR pictures come from a free public QR image service (quickchart.io by default, or api.qrserver.com). It only receives the label text, never your quantities, costs or suppliers, and it needs an internet connection to draw them.

FAQ

Questions, answered straight

Is it a subscription?

No. It's a one-time download from Etsy. The file is yours to keep; there's no monthly fee.

Does it work in Google Sheets?

Yes. It's a real .xlsx file with live formulas and no macros. In Google Sheets use File → Import → Upload, then click Allow access on the yellow banner once so the QR codes can load.

Do the QR codes work in Excel?

In Excel 365, yes: they're drawn with the built-in IMAGE() function. In older Excel (2016–2021) the QR cells show a note instead; click the QR image link on each row to open the QR as a picture, or print the Labels tab from Google Sheets. The code text always prints.

Can I scan a label with my phone?

Your phone camera reads the code on the label (for example BOX-001), and you search for it in the sheet. The Quick Start also shows an optional free Google Form set-up, so a scan opens a form with the code filled in. It isn't an app: scanning doesn't change the stock count by itself.

How many items does it hold?

500 items, 100 locations and boxes, 2,000 stock movements, 30 suppliers and 20 lines per purchase order. The Reorder List shows up to 60 items at a time.

Does it track sales and profit?

No. It tracks stock: what you have, where it is, what came in and went out, and what to reorder. Stock value uses the unit cost you type. If you need revenue, cost of goods sold and profit per product, pick one of the trackers named above that lists those.

Is this accounting or tax software?

No. It's a record-keeping tool, not accounting, tax or financial advice. The stock value isn't an inventory valuation method for your books.
