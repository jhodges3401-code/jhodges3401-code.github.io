FAQ

Questions, answered straight

What should a client onboarding checklist include?

At minimum: scope and price in writing, the deposit or first invoice, contacts and the channel you'll use, access to their files and accounts, a kickoff call that sets the first milestone and who approves it, and a first progress note in week one. This file has 26 items in five sections, A to E, in that order.

When do I send each kickoff email?

As the file notes on each one: the scope confirmation after the discovery call and before any work starts, the welcome email right after they say yes, and the kickoff recap within a few hours of the kickoff call.

Is this a contract?

No. It's a checklist and emails, not a legal contract. The first item asks for scope confirmed in writing (email or SOW); use your own agreement for the legal terms.

Do I need a CRM to use it?

No. A few items say “CRM” and “invoice log”; a spreadsheet, a notes file or whatever client tracker you already use works the same way.

Is there a free client onboarding checklist?

Yes, and some are good; see the comparison on this page. Freshlance's free checklist is longer than ours (twelve sections), and OnboardHive has ten free onboarding emails. This kit is for people who want one short printable checklist plus the three emails in one Word file.

What format is it?

A print-ready PDF (US Letter, prints fine on A4) and an editable Word file (.docx), which also opens in Google Docs and Pages. Everything in [brackets] is yours to replace.
