How one planting is worked out

  • Sow in tray = plant-out minus 5 weeks in tray; harvest starts 68 days (days to maturity) after plant-out and runs 2 weeks
  • Row feet = 1 bed × 100 ft × 4 rows = 400; plants = 400 × 12 ÷ 6 in spacing = 800
  • Seeds = 800 × (1 + 10% overplant) ÷ 80% germination ÷ (1 − 10% tray loss) = 1,222
  • Seed Order adds all 8: 9,778 seeds, so 10 packets of 1,000 at $4.95 = $49.50 (made-up packet price)

How the math is checked

Every formula tested against a separate model

Before release, a test script rebuilt every result outside the spreadsheet: 4,242 comparisons on the example file and 2,896 on the blank one, plus 24 edited and random test plans with 122,509 comparisons against an independent model, 0 failures. Negative tests made sure every warning fires: bed conflicts, beds past your bed count, plantings before the safe date or past first fall frost, and planner and library errors.

Where the starter values come from

University of Missouri Extension G6201 and the Territorial Seed Company planting chart (days to maturity, spacing, seed rate, yield), Purdue Extension HO-186 (frost classes), Rutgers NJAES FS787 (weeks in tray) and the federal minimum germination standards in 7 CFR 201.31 (your packet often tests higher). Rows per bed and harvest weeks are our own estimates for a 30-inch bed. Check them all against your seed packets and change any that don't fit your farm.

Questions

Does it work in Google Sheets?

Yes. It's a regular .xlsx file with standard formulas and no macros. Open it in Excel 2016 or newer, or in Google Sheets use File → Import → Upload.

My frost dates are different. What changes?

Type your two frost dates on Farm Settings. Each crop's safe plant-out date and the frost warnings update right away, so you can see which first dates to move. Planting dates don't move by themselves: change a crop's first date on the Succession Planner and all its later successions move with it.

Can I add crops that aren't in the starter list?

Yes. There are 26 empty Crop Library rows; fill in the numbers from your seed packet or catalog and the crop shows up in the Succession Planner.

Are the starter numbers right for my farm?

They're averages from extension guides and a seed company chart, for other regions and varieties, and rows per bed and harvest weeks are our own estimates for a 30-inch bed. Treat them as a starting point and change any number that doesn't match your seed packets or your own records.

How big a farm does it handle?

Up to 40 beds, 40 crop plans and 300 plantings over a 40-week window. Every bed is treated as the same length, which you set on Farm Settings.
