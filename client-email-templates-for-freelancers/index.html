Before you hit send

The last page is a five-point check:

  • The person asked for this conversation, or is a client or warm lead
  • No fake deadlines or "only one spot left" pressure
  • No legal, medical or guaranteed-results claims
  • Every [bracket] is filled in
  • You'd be happy to receive this email yourself

FAQ

Questions, answered straight

Are there free client email templates for freelancers?

Yes, and some are better for certain jobs. Gatilab's free Client Communication Swipe File has 14 scripts for hard conversations this pack doesn't cover, like a missed deadline, your own mistake or a client who has gone quiet. Begin Things has a free browser tool that drafts ten common client messages, in a friendly or formal tone, as an email or a WhatsApp message. Bryn Taylor shares 8 short ones, including cold outreach. This pack is for people who want one email for each step of a normal project, in order, with a when-to-send line and subject line on every one, as a Word file.

Are these the same emails as in the Notion client CRM?

Yes. They're word for word the same 15 emails as the email page in our Notion client CRM template (Freelance Client OS). This pack adds a when-to-send line on each email and a before-you-send checklist, as Word and PDF. If you only want the emails, this is the cheaper way. You don't need both.

Do they overlap with your other email packs?

Partly. Emails 1 to 3 are the same as the three emails in the client onboarding checklist pack, and emails 6 to 8 are the same as the first three emails in the payment reminder pack. That pack adds a fourth, firmer email for about 14 days late and an invoice tracker. The rate increase pack is different wording: a proposal email, a rate increase notice, a polite pass and a pushback reply. This pack has one shorter rate raise email.

Can I edit them?

Yes. The Word file is fully editable and also opens in Google Docs and Pages. The PDF is for printing or reading.

Are they for cold emailing?

No. They're for one-to-one emails with clients and people who contacted you, not mass mailings. The file says so on the first page.

Is there legal wording in them?

No. They're plain business emails, not legal notices or contract terms. Email 8 mentions pausing new work 'per our agreement', so only send that line if your agreement says so.
