How to choose

Does the report hold everything a PCVR needs?

BESA lists before and after photos from matching positions, WFTT or DTT grease readings against the 200 micron mean, a summary of works, access limits, system drawings and a hygiene certificate (BESA). Ask each vendor for a sample report and tick these off. If you’re in BESCA’s VHE scheme, ask whether the report fits what BESCA audits.

Whole-firm or per-engineer pricing?

Pricing models differ a lot. For a firm with 4 engineers, at list prices: SMART Reports £90/mo (one report type, all users), Rugged Data £140/mo plus set-up from £495, Joblogic Standard from £196/mo billed annually, and Flow Mobile £400/mo plus VAT. TR19Flow prices by active sites and users instead, from £39 for 8 sites. Count your engineers and sites before you compare.

Will it work with no signal?

Plant rooms, basements and roofs often have poor signal. Flow Mobile, Rugged Data, Footprint WFM and ServiceM8 list offline use. For the others we couldn’t find it stated, so ask before you buy.

Does it know when the next clean is due?

Missed re-cleans are lost work. SMART Reports sets the next clean date from kitchen usage and TR19Flow runs a renewal dashboard with client alerts. With general apps you’d set up recurring jobs yourself.

Can you bring your records across?

SMART Reports says clients, sites, systems and clean dates import from a spreadsheet or a Flow Mobile export; old reports and photos don’t move (source). Ask any vendor what moves and what stays behind.

FAQ

Is TR19 software a legal requirement?

No. TR19® Grease is BESA’s specification, not a law, and no rule says you must use software to meet it. What the law does say, under the Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order, is that the responsible person must assess and reduce fire risks in non-domestic premises, and BESA notes that insurers often want proof that kitchen extract ductwork is cleaned to a recognised standard (BESA). Software only records the work; it doesn’t make a system compliant.

What goes in a post-clean verification report (PCVR)?

BESA lists before and after photos taken from matching positions, Wet Film Thickness Test (WFTT) or Deposit Thickness Test (DTT) readings showing grease below the 200 micron limit, a written summary of the work, notes on access limits or areas needing attention, system drawings showing what was cleaned, and a hygiene certificate with the date, location and technician’s name (BESA). Check that a tool’s report has a field for each of these before you pay.

How often must kitchen extract ductwork be cleaned under TR19 Grease?

BESA gives minimum intervals by daily cooking hours: heavy use (12 to 16 hours a day) every 3 months, moderate use (6 to 12 hours) every 6 months, and light use (2 to 6 hours) every 12 months. Grease should not exceed a mean of 200 microns between cleans, and areas at 500 microns or more need spot cleaning straight away. A competent person’s risk assessment can call for more frequent cleans (BESA).

What is BESCA Ventilation Hygiene Elite (VHE)?

BESA describes VHE as a scheme run by BESCA, the UK’s largest independently audited scheme for kitchen extract cleaning. Members must follow BESCA’s code of conduct and TR19® Grease, BESCA audits their post-clean reports, and each clean registered on the BESCA portal produces a BESCA certificate (BESA). If you’re a member, ask each vendor whether its report fits what BESCA checks.

What’s the cheapest TR19 reporting app?

Of the extract-specific tools that publish prices, TR19Flow Solo is lowest at £39 a month, but it covers 8 active sites and 1 user. SMART Reports is £90 a month with every user included, so it gets cheaper per engineer as you grow. ServiceM8 has a free plan, but it’s a general job app with no TR19 template mentioned.
