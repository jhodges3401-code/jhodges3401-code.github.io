Privacy note

Last updated October 17, 2026. Applies to all pages on orvianmarketplace.com, including the product demos and the software guides.

What we measure

We use Google Analytics 4 to count page views and to see which products people click to buy. When you click a buy link that goes to Gumroad, we record a buy_click event with the product name and the link. Google Analytics sets first-party cookies and receives standard browser data (pages viewed, referrer, approximate location from your IP, device and browser type). We don't send your name, email or anything you type into our tools.

What we don't collect

  • No ads and no ad retargeting on our pages.
  • Data you enter in our browser tools (Proposeroo Desk, Proposeroo, PeelPixel, the calculators) stays in your browser. PeelPixel images are processed on your device and never uploaded.
  • Okayline and Kudoshelf run in your own GitHub account; we never see that data.
  • We don't sell personal data.

Purchases

Checkout happens on Gumroad, which has its own privacy policy. We receive the order details Gumroad shares with sellers so we can deliver your product and handle refunds.

Opting out

You can block Google Analytics with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on, a content blocker, or your browser's tracking protection. Our pages work the same either way. See how Google uses data from sites that use its services.

Questions

Reply to any Gumroad receipt or open an issue on GitHub.
